# Code of conduct

We want foxcast to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background or level of experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Criticise ideas, code and evidence, never people.
- Say plainly when you are unsure, and welcome being shown wrong. This project's whole method depends on it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing anyone's private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the contact listed on the repository. Maintainers may remove contributions or block participants who break this code.
